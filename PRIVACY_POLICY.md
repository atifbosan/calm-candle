# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 7, 2026
**Last Updated:** November 23, 2026

## Calm Candle (Candle Light: Sleep Sounds)

This Privacy Policy explains how **Calm Candle**, published on Google Play as **"Candle Light: Sleep Sounds"** (the "App"), handles information. The App is published by **TeraBit Tech** ("we", "our" or "us").

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Please also read our [Terms of Service](terms-and-conditions.html).

---

## Summary

* We do **not** ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account.
* Your settings (chosen scene, sounds, mixes, timer and preferences) are stored **only on your device**.
* The free version shows ads from **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads (see *Advertising* below).
* Subscriptions are handled entirely by **Google Play**. We never see or store your payment details.
* The microphone is used **only** if you turn on "Blow to extinguish", is processed on your device in real time, and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

---

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect or store:

* Names, email addresses or phone numbers
* Payment card or bank details
* Precise (GPS) location
* Contacts, photos, videos, files or messages
* Audio recordings

---

## Information stored on your device

The App saves your preferences locally on your device, for example the candle scene you chose, flame size, sound volumes, saved sound mixes, favourites, timer settings and whether onboarding has been completed. This information never leaves your device through us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

---

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App displays ads served by **Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC. To show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

* **Device identifiers**, such as your device's advertising ID and app set ID
* **Approximate location** inferred from your IP address
* **App activity**, such as ad views, clicks and interactions
* **Diagnostics**, such as crash and performance information related to ads

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies:

* Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
* How Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization at any time in your device settings (on most Android devices: **Settings → Google → Ads** or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

**Premium subscribers do not see ads**, and no ad requests are made while a subscription is active.

---

## Subscriptions and purchases (Google Play)

Premium subscriptions are purchased and managed through **Google Play Billing**. Google processes your payment. We do not receive or store your payment information.

To unlock premium content, the App receives the status of your purchase from Google Play (for example, which plan is active and whether it is still valid) and keeps this status on your device. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play Store under **Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**.

Google's privacy policy applies to purchases made through Google Play: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

---

## Microphone

The App requests microphone access **only** when you turn on the **"Blow to extinguish"** feature.

* The microphone is used solely to measure the sound level in real time, so that blowing towards your phone can extinguish the on-screen candle.
* Processing happens entirely **on your device**.
* We do **not** record, store or transmit any audio.
* If you do not turn the feature on, the microphone is never accessed. You can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings (**Settings → Apps → Calm Candle → Permissions**). The rest of the App keeps working normally.

---

## Other permissions

* **Internet / network state:** to load ads, verify subscriptions with Google Play and open links.
* **Vibration:** for optional haptic feedback.
* **Keep screen on (wake lock):** so the candle stays visible while you use it.

---

## Data security

Your preferences are kept in the App's private storage on your device. Communication with Google services (ads and Google Play) is encrypted in transit by those services. We do not operate our own servers that collect personal data.

---

## Data retention and deletion

We do not hold personal data about you on our own systems, so there is nothing for us to delete. You can remove all data the App keeps on your device by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. To manage data held by Google for advertising, use the controls described in the *Advertising* section and your Google account settings.

---

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help address it.

---

## Not a health or medical app

The App provides decorative candle visuals and ambient sounds for entertainment and atmosphere only. It does not collect health information and does not provide health, medical or wellness services.
